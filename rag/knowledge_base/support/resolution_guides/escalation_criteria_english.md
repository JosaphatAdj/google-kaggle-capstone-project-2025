# Escalation Criteria - Customer Support

## Technical Cases (Immediate Escalation)
- Lidar not rotating
- Anti-drop sensor faulty despite cleaning
- Swollen or very hot battery
- Suspected motherboard issue
- Corrupted firmware (repeated E08)
- LawnBot GPS impossible to calibrate

## Customer Cases (HITL Escalation)
- Very dissatisfied customer (intense negative sentiment)
- Aggressive language
- Refund request
- Threat of complaint / negative review

## Administrative Cases
- Complex warranty claims
- Undelivered orders
- Incorrect billing
