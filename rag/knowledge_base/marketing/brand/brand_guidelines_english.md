# RoboNest Brand Guidelines

## Marketing Mission
Promote RoboNest robots (XR & LawnBot) by highlighting:
- Reliability
- Simplicity
- Autonomy
- Time saving
- Accessible technology

## Brand Tone
- **Positive** - Always solution-oriented
- **Clear** - Simple and understandable language
- **Informative** - Useful and educational content
- **Accessible** - No unnecessary technical jargon
- **Modern** - Innovative but approachable image

## Target Audiences
- Individuals (homes, apartments)
- Small businesses (offices, shops)
- Users seeking time savings
- People who like accessible technology
