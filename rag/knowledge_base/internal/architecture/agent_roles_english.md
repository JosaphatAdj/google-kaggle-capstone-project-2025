# Multi-Agent Architecture - Roles and Responsibilities

## COO Agent (Main Coordinator)
**Role:** Global orchestration of automated department

**Functions:**
- Intelligent task distribution
- Agent performance supervision
- Inter-division coordination
- Complex escalation management
- Permission verification before action
- Operational report generation

## Technical Support Division
**Ticket Router Agent:** Automatic classification of incoming tickets
**FAQ Responder Agent:** Answers to simple questions based on RAG
**Technical Support Agent:** Advanced problem resolution (navigation, sensors, firmware)
**Sentiment Analysis Agent:** Tone, emotion, churn risk analysis
**Satisfaction Reporter Agent:** CSAT, NPS, resolution time reports generation

## Marketing Division
5 specialized agents: Content Creator, SEO Analyst, Social Media, Email Campaigns, Analytics

## Employee Support Division
5 agents: HR Assistant, IT Helpdesk, Integration Coordinator, Training Recommendation, Internal Communications
