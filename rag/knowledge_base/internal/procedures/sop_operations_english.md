# Standard Operating Procedures - Operations

## Internal Request Management
All internal requests must:
- Be categorized (IT, HR, logistics, tools...)
- Be recorded in internal tool
- Receive initial response < 12h
- Be closed with user confirmation

## Internal Incident Management
**Incident protocol:**
1. Problem identification
2. Quick initial diagnosis
3. Escalation if wide impact (HITL mandatory)
4. Clear internal communication
5. Resolution report

## Archiving Policies
- **Operational documents**: 5 years retention
- **Internal communications**: 2 years
- **Agent logs**: 1 year minimum

## Company Structure
**Main divisions:**
- Customer Support
- Employee Support (HR + Internal IT)
- Marketing
- Technical Department (Robotics R&D)
- Operations & Quality
