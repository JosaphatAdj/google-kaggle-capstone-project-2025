# Brand Guide & Communication Tone

## Communication Principles
- **Clear** - Simple and direct message
- **Professional** - Respectful and competent
- **Empathetic** - Understanding of needs
- **Solution-oriented** - Focus on resolution
- **No unnecessary jargon** - Accessible to all

## Company Voice
- Positive and caring
- Never condescending
- Transparent and honest
- Focus on immediate help
- Encouragement of customer satisfaction

## Internal Writing Style
- Short and concise messages
- Simple paragraphs
- Step-by-step instructions
- Always propose an action at the end
