# Internal Communications Guide

## Communication Types
- HR announcements
- Organizational changes
- System/IT updates
- New employees
- Internal projects
- Internal newsletter

## Recommended Announcement Structure
1. **Context** (1-2 sentences)
2. **Change details**
3. **Impact on teams**
4. **Implementation date**
5. **Contact or internal resource**

## Communication Rules
- Professional and positive tone
- No unnecessary sensitive details
- Always clarify next steps
- Encourage questions
- Secure internal links only
