# Preventive Maintenance XR Series

## Every 2 weeks
- Clean sensors with dry cloth
- Empty dust container
- Clean side & main brush

## Every month
- Replace HEPA filter
- Check battery condition

## Every 6 months
- Firmware update
- Drive wheel inspection

## Standard Diagnostic Procedure
1. Identify model (XR25 or XR30)
2. Check power and charging
3. Read error codes via app/sounds
4. Check consumables (filter, brush, container)
5. Clean sensors (Lidar, anti-drop, dust)
6. Perform forced restart (POWER 10 sec)
7. Test manual mode
